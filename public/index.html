        How to Play

        Objective

        Fight other players in the arena. Land hits to deal damage, avoid being hit, and score the most points before the round timer runs out.

        Controls — Desktop

        • WASD / Arrow Keys — move
        • Mouse — aim
        • Left Click — ranged attack
        • Right Click / Space — melee strike (short range, hits harder)

        Controls — Mobile

        • Left joystick — move
        • Right side drag — aim
        • ⚔ button — ranged attack
        • 👊 button — melee strike

        Power-ups

        • Speed Boost — move faster for a short time
        • Health Restore — instantly heal
        • Shield — reduce incoming damage
        • Rapid Attack — fire much faster
        • Damage Boost — hit harder

        Bots

        No one else online? The lobby host can add AI bots to fill the arena — click + Bot before starting.

        Kill Streaks

        Chain kills without dying for bonus points and an on-screen callout — Double Kill, Triple Kill, Rampage, Unstoppable, Godlike.

        Armory

        Winning a round earns Credits. Spend them in the 🛒 Armory from the main menu on new weapons (Rapid Fire, Shotgun, Railgun, Grenade Launcher) and permanent character upgrades (Vitality, Agility, Reflexes, Brawler). Your loadout carries into every match you join.

        The Grenade Launcher deals splash damage to everyone near where it lands — including you, if you're standing too close.
